0.9.53: Safeguard for LG TVs where the PS5 picture did not appear and the screen stayed black once translation started. The sound steps added in 0.9.47-0.9.48 no longer run before the picture arrives; they are tried only after the picture has played for a few seconds, and if they break it they are turned off and the picture is reopened the old way. Media focus and TV unmute are off by default.
